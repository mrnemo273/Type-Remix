Grex Sans is IBM Plex Sans with grain pressed into the outlines: edges that wobble, bites out of the ink, specks thrown off and knockouts inside the strokes. Drag the pad. Switch to As drawn to see what the grain is made of.

Proof 1 — Size

The grain scales with the type. Set it by hand, or let it follow the size so text stays clean and headlines carry the texture.

Proof 2 — Weight × grain

Four weights at three grain levels. Above Regular the grain grows slower than the stroke, so Bold stays a word and doesn't turn into a smudge.

Proof 3 — Features

Alternates and figure styles inherited from Plex Sans. Changed glyphs in magenta.
