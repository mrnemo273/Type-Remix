Size

Grain that knows its size.

Because the grain lives in the outlines it scales with the type. Tie the axis to font size and text stays clean while headlines carry the texture. This ladder does exactly that.

Weight × grain

Bold stays a word.

Seven weights across, three grain levels down. Above Regular the grain grows slower than the stroke, so the heavy weights keep their shape.

In use

Out in the world.

Fourteen jobs for one typeface: packaging, posters, a book, a ticket, signage, a menu and an app. Each card notes the weight and grain it's set at.

Overprint

Ink over ink.

Grex Sans was made for overprinting. Layer two or three inks and the knockouts in each pass let the colour underneath show through, the way a riso or a screenprint would. Six compositions, each a different way of stacking the passes.

Print run

One to twelve.

The same word from the first impression to the last. Each is one setting of the grain axis; nothing here is an image.

Features

Alternates, kept.

Everything Plex Sans offers carries over: single-storey a and g, two zeros, oldstyle figures, fractions, super- and subscripts. Changed glyphs in pink.
